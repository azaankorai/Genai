FRAMEFORGE

Privacy notice

FrameForge uses your email address to create and secure your account. We store your prompts, generation settings, credit activity, subscription references, and generated media to provide the service.

Providers and payments

Sign-in emails are delivered by Resend. Prompts and generation settings are sent to fal.ai and the selected model provider. Payments are processed by Stripe; FrameForge does not store your card number. Hosting infrastructure stores the account database and generated media. Each provider processes data under its own terms.

Private media

Your library and media downloads require an authenticated session for your account. They are not listed publicly. People with access to your account or device can access your media. Service administrators can access stored data when needed for operations or support.

Cookies and browser storage

Essential, HTTP-only cookies secure your session and bind sign-in links to the requesting browser. Your session expires after seven days. A short-lived browser session-storage entry prevents duplicate generation requests. We do not include advertising or analytics trackers in this version.

Retention and deletion

Generated media stays in your account until you delete it or request account removal. Deleting a generation removes the stored media; an internal request record and credit ledger remain to prevent duplicate charges and resolve payment disputes. Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes. Security rate-limit records expire automatically. Backups and external providers may retain copies under their own retention schedules.

Your requests

Contact support to request account closure, access, correction, or removal of personal data. Payment records may need to be retained for legal and accounting obligations. Avoid including confidential information or personal data about others in prompts unless you have the necessary rights.

Operator and support details are available through the studio’s Support link. If support details have not been configured, this service is still in pre-launch setup.
